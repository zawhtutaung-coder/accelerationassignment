Calculation used in this interactive comparison

The launch force and vehicle mass give a force-based acceleration. The program compares this value with the entered initial acceleration and uses the lower value as the usable starting acceleration. Acceleration is then gradually reduced as vehicle speed increases to represent the increasing difficulty of accelerating at high speed.

aforce = Flaunch / m

a(v) = a0 / (1 + k v²)

Because the same model is used for both drivetrains, AWD will reach the target sooner whenever its usable acceleration is high enough to overcome its additional mass.
